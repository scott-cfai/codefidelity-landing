About the Founder

Turning two decades of enterprise code intelligence into the next generation of software understanding.

Why I founded CodeFidelity AI

The software industry is entering a new era.

AI can generate code faster than ever before, but organizations still need to understand:

  • What code exists
  • Where it came from
  • Whether it is secure
  • Which releases contain it
  • How it impacts the rest of the organization

These are the same challenges I spent years solving at enterprise scale.

CodeFidelity AI was founded to bring that experience into a platform built for the AI era — helping organizations build software they can understand, trust, and maintain.

Looking forward

My mission is simple:

Help organizations answer one of the most important questions in modern software engineering: “Do we really know what's in our code?”

That mission is the foundation of CodeFidelity AI.
